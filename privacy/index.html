Shotwell, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Shotwell", "we", "us"), provides annotation and data-quality services for robotics data. This policy covers the shotwell.ai website. Our labeling platform presents its own privacy notice and agreements inside the platform.

Customer content. Videos, sensor data, and related material that our customers give us to label are processed on those customers' behalf and are governed by our agreements with them, not by this policy. Questions about that content belong with the customer that collected it.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide, operate, secure, and improve the website; to communicate with you; to analyze usage of our website; and to comply with law. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to help us understand site traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Where data is processed

We are based in the United States and process and store data there.

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, security, and record-keeping needs. After that, we delete or de-identify it.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for the information we hold, including encryption in transit and role-based access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by writing to team@shotwell.ai. We will verify the request and respond as required by applicable law. The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
