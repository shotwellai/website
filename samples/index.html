What changed

The hero was unreadable because the tone mapping was wrong, not the idea. Your footage averages about 90/255 luma — genuinely dark — so a raw brightness-to-dot-size mapping produced mostly tiny dots. The renderer now stretches levels (0.04–0.85) and lifts midtones (gamma 0.62) before sizing dots, and the pitch dropped from 10px to 6–7px. The second fix was structural: no type sits on the dot field in any of the three variants.

Light mode is a full inversion, not a tint. On a light ground the polarity flips — dark pixels become the big dots. The saturated fields keep their colour in both themes, since holding a chromatic ground is the whole point of them.
