Where these colours came from.

You asked whether the four below came from the board. They did not — I took them from your footage and from computer-vision convention. So I went back and clustered the board itself: 170,908 chromatic pixels, page-white and near-black excluded. Yellow-gold is 44% of the chromatic content and orange is 3.6%. Orange was never on your board. It came from the logo flame, and I carried it across without checking.
