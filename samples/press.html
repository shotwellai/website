Shotwell raises $6M to make robot data trustworthy

Shotwell today announced a $6M seed round to expand the quality layer it builds for robotics training data — dense annotation, automated review, and an audit trail for every frame that reaches a model.

Robotics teams increasingly collect more demonstration footage than they can verify. A single manipulation episode can carry thousands of frames, each needing masks, keypoints, and trajectory labels that agree with one another across time.

Shotwell's pipeline scores its own output before delivery. Every episode ships with per-frame agreement metrics, a list of the checks that ran, and the frames those checks rejected.

The company works with teams building manipulation policies for household and industrial tasks, and will use the round to grow its review infrastructure and annotation tooling.
